## Non-negotiable boundaries

No completion work may create a parallel provider, marketplace, payment, request, coordination, notification, or memory engine. Public listings remain distinct from verified providers. Provider availability remains distinct from opening hours, quote, payment, dispatch and fulfilment. LLM text, UI state, client-supplied coordinates, a Trust Score, a Point balance, a subscription tier, or a mutable profile flag cannot alone become high-impact authority.
