# Welcome to Kurukoo: Wake up. Get going.

> **Kurukoo is a service that helps people get things done.**
>
> You tell Kurukoo what you need, want, notice, or are worried about.
>
> Kurukoo understands the situation, works out what it can do, takes appropriate action, gets help from people or services when necessary, keeps you informed, and remembers what matters.

## Start with the need

You do not need to know which category your problem belongs to. Tell Kurukoo what is happening in your own words.

“I'm running late and need to get to the airport.”

“My laptop is slow. Check it.”

“Find somewhere good to eat tonight.”

“Remind me about this tomorrow.”

“Keep an eye on this.”

Kurukoo works out what information and next step are useful.

## Kurukoo can help directly

When the available device, service or connected resource exposes what Kurukoo needs, it can inspect, explain, diagnose, organise, monitor or take an appropriate authorised action. It should not send you to a person unnecessarily.

If a problem needs physical access, specialist expertise or another participant, Kurukoo can help find and coordinate that help and carry the useful context forward.

## One service, many ways to help

Kurukoo can work across everyday assistance, services, products, places, providers, connected devices, memory, tasks, reminders, discovery, agents and physical fulfilment. The user experience remains one service even when different tools, people or external systems are involved.

## You stay informed and in control

Kurukoo should tell you what it knows, what it needs from you, what it is doing, and what happened. Important actions can require your permission. If something cannot be established or completed, Kurukoo should say so rather than pretending.

When you are away, supported notifications and channels can bring important updates to you. When you return, the same work can continue from its existing context rather than starting over.

## Built to grow by helping people

Kurukoo's capabilities are not intended to be a fixed list of categories. As new ways of helping become possible, they should become part of the same service rather than turning into disconnected products.

The goal is simple: **tell Kurukoo what you need, and let Kurukoo help you get it done.**
